# Transaction Rolodex

September 15, 2026. The motion study now hosts three focused decision queues: validate transfers, tag income and tag expenses. It retains the Rolodex, paired ticks, staggered filtering and envelope completion. All records and decisions are illustrative and in memory; the app and real ledger are untouched.

Build with `npm run prototype:ingest`. The existing private design host serves it at `/lab/`; run `npm run docs:serve` if needed. This folder contains the entire interaction and can be removed independently. Build output is ignored under `private/ingest-lab`. No production component, data store or migration depends on it.

## Current decision trial

- 67 illustrative bank records: the earlier 60 faces plus seven records forming three transfer questions (exact pair, fee, competing candidates). Start with Transfers, then switch freely to Income and Expenses.
- Transfer candidates are deliberately authored fixtures, not a new matching algorithm. Candidate selection and Link are separate; differences require acknowledgement. A linked pair leaves both tagging queues. Not a transfer releases the related records to tagging; the unchosen counterpart of an ambiguous link becomes available as an expense.
- Income has flat tag choices and search. Expenses have category tabs, leaf tags and search across categories. Multiple tags split evenly at first, with exact-cent inputs and a two-tag divider. Save is disabled until positive portions sum to the full amount. Reimbursement/refund tags label receipts only; they do not create an income purpose, allocation or deduction.
- The specific Save/Link action commits the practice decision before the envelope plays. Generic Done is removed. Later/navigation leave an item pending. Undo restores the most recent decision and its queue; Reset or refresh resets all practice decisions. Tags and links are not persisted.
- Desktop pairs the roll with a decision panel. Phone uses a shorter roll and a panel below it; the panel repeats the transaction name/amount for context while scrolling. More complex splits may require vertical scrolling.
- Current checks: `node --test prototypes/ingest/practice.test.mjs` and `node prototypes/ingest/check-decisions.cjs`. They cover queue relationships, fee acknowledgement, ambiguous links, dismissal, tag type/cents validation, undo, later, reset, reduced motion and four viewport sizes. Earlier check scripts target the former motion-only controls/dataset and are historical regression references.
- Next evidence is the user's reaction to these three surfaces: whether each question is obvious, choices are reachable on a phone, and completion means what they expect. Passing checks does not establish comfort or production readiness.

## Retained motion

- Filter changes stagger individual cards out right, then in from the left. Immediate cards are spaced 42ms apart, and each card's two tick marks share its delay, travel and fade. Outer ticks continue the cascade with shorter spacing. Per-item exits take 160ms and arrivals 220ms (roughly 650ms with stagger). The scene and rail labels remain stationary. During flight the rails allow horizontal overflow; explicit opacity preserves their vertical feather. The queue changes only after all outgoing items are invisible. A current match is retained when possible. Rapid choices during exit update the pending result; choices during arrival leave from each item's current pose. Empty states also slide/fade; reduced motion is immediate. Ordinary navigation is locked during filtering; filters remain usable. Saving retains its 750ms envelope animation. `check-filter-slide.cjs` checks stagger, matching tick/card motion, overflow, cleanup and interrupted transitions; `check-filters.cjs` covers combined filters, empty results, completed-card retention and responsive views.
- The seal counter-rotates the folded paper so its checkmark settles upright; its SVG path is centered in both axes. The user removed the confetti trial. `check-duration.cjs` verifies the configured 750ms timing; `check-finish.cjs` verifies seal alignment and cleanup.
- The user selected a fixed 750ms total fold → stamp → send-off sequence. The speed slider is removed and old browser preferences are ignored. Relative phase timing, departing ticks and the completion gap spring share the same rate; normal scrolling remains unchanged. Reduced-motion confirmation keeps its brief fade.
- Both sides of every flap inherit the source card's corner radius and clip their contents. Preserve those cutouts from the first replacement frame through folding; a square backing must never flash behind the rounded front. `node prototypes/ingest/check-corners.cjs` checks all four corner pixels on phone and desktop.
- The envelope uses four triangular corner panels hinged on the edges of an inscribed rectangle. Their actual paper edges create the final seams; no triangle or seam overlay appears after folding. The sheet turns gently level as it folds. The selected tick on each rail lifts outside its mask and shares the envelope's departure keyframes, then disappears with the sent card. Other ticks retain their gap-closing motion.
- The user selected fixed 2px blur / 70% apparent opacity for neighbouring cards. Tune and preference persistence are removed; old browser settings are ignored. The focused card remains crisp and opaque.
- Saving folds the left and right edges inward, then closes the top and bottom folds, stamps a checkmark and sends it right with a fade while the next card uses the existing roll spring. Navigation and duplicate sends are locked during the sequence; Done waits for the roll to settle. Reduced motion uses a brief confirmation fade. Sent cards leave the in-memory stack. The upper neighbour stays at its exact pose while a separate spring closes the removed slot from below; the departed card is never restored. Refresh replays the study; Replay cards resets the completed sample set. No completion state or financial decision is persisted.

- Vertical direct manipulation of the card stack, momentum projection on release, critically damped snap to a card.
- A monotone, bounded card path replaces the former square-root spacing expansion, removing the neighbouring card's outward kick and reversal. The spring uses actual first-frame time, a softer response and retained velocity when a new index tap interrupts motion; mid-handoff clearance remains protected.
- The user explicitly approved the smoothness. Preserve the spring, gesture response and position path. A 68° resting fold was too flat and lost the roll feel; use a gentler 51° fold, close to the original 43°. The small extra fold eases in after the midpoint for a modest height reduction without shrinking the selected card.
- Wheel/trackpad input with a quiet-period snap. Horizontal gestures and browser zoom are not intercepted.
- A short-tick index beside the stack. Length and brightness follow distance from the continuous position, matching the reference. Tap a mark or scrub the rail; both move the same spring as the cards.
- Matching interactive tick indexes flank both card edges. During a handoff the two readable faces separate before changing layer order. Neighbour softening uses an 30% dark wash over an opaque face plus 2px blur at rest, easing from a fully sharp focal area using the shared position. Actual card opacity stays at 1: the earlier 82% group opacity exposed tucked-away cards through the moving faces. No independent fade timer. Beyond the immediate neighbours, deeper cards shrink inward to become occluded; they do not create corner wedges or a ghosted tail. The resting stack remains compact.
- Click a visible neighboring card; previous/next buttons; Arrow keys, Page keys, Home/End.
- Bounded ends, interrupted animation pickup, pointer cancellation returns to the starting card, resizing maintains selection.
- Reduced motion removes the 3D spring animation. Semantic listbox and polite settled-card announcement remain.

The illustrative records and decisions are session-only. The initial selection is the first incoming transfer candidate. Nothing connects to real accounts or the financial service.
