# Organize desk

October 2, 2026. Organize is now one page: the ledger itself, across every account, newest first. It replaces the inbox-and-sheet allocation editor and the separate Transfers tab. The user asked for something convenient and intuitive with no taught steps, and chose immediate saves over a draft-then-save contract. Transfer pairing settings moved to Settings → Transfers.

## What the page is

- **One list.** Every active, non-zero transaction, grouped by day. Filters: All, Unsorted (with a count), Money in, Money out, Between accounts, plus account chips and a search over readable and original descriptions. Rows render 150 at a time with Show more.
- **The shape strip.** Each row carries its amount as a strip of parts: tags in their inherited colors, repayments in the person's color, a linked transfer with its fee, and a hatched gap for whatever is still unsorted. An unsorted month reads as a column of gaps.
- **Rows open in place as a parts-first card (October 2, 2026, after the open-card study).** The header row stays: name, account, date and original description, the strip, the amount. Below it, the left side is the describe box and a list of typed part rows; the right side holds fixed slots for Split, Event and Rule, with a quiet Details disclosure (original description, account, date, original file, transfer tolerance with a link to Settings → Transfers).
- **Four natures, read off the parts.** Every bank line is a Move (between your own accounts), a Spend (tags), a Receive (income tags) or an Offset (a person settling what they owe). The card never asks for the nature; each part row is labelled with it: Spend/Receive for tags, Offset for a settlement, Move for a linked transfer, Fee and Extra for a transfer difference. Each row has an amount box and a remove control; an Unsorted row shows the gap.
- **One describe box.** Type what the line is. Results are grouped by what the line can be, in this order: Same money (likely transfer twins), Settles up (people, for money in, with what they owe you), Shared with and Paid a person (people, for money out), Tags for that direction, Events (with the event's participants), Own accounts (accounts with no twin, shown disabled with the reason). Enter takes the top result. With no query the box leads with people who owe you money and tags used before for the same description. Grouping and keyboard order use the same ordered list.

## What each gesture does

- **Instant.** A change shows in the list the moment you make it. The save runs in the background through a serial queue, each against the row's latest version, and the ledger reloads afterwards. A failed save shows its error and reloads the truth. The app shell is not asked to refresh, so there is no "Saving…" pause.
- **Shared with…** On an expense, typing a person's name offers "Shared with Sam" (above "Paid Sam back", which records the payee without a split). The first pick splits the amount evenly between you and the people named. The Split slot on the open row shows every person as a toggle, an editable share for each person in the split, what they have settled or still owe, your own share, and Split evenly. This is the existing agreed-shares model (`review.shares`), so Dashboard and Events see it too. Removing the last person clears the shares.
- **Events carry the split.** An event names its participants (Events → edit → Split with). An expense that joins the event takes the event's even split, marked as following it; when the event's participants change, every member expense that still follows it is rewritten in one versioned pass. Editing a share on such an expense makes it a custom split, which the event never overwrites; "Follow <event> again" returns to the event's split. Removing a participant who has repaid a member expense is refused. A transaction belongs to one event: choosing another moves it.
- **People carry a balance.** What a person owes you is the sum of their shares on your expenses minus what they have settled. On money in, "Sam settles up" shows that balance; picking it draws the balance down by the receipt's unsorted amount (or less, when the balance is smaller), attributed to Sam's oldest open shares first. The card shows one Offset row, "Sam settled", with the balance before and after, an amount box that extends or trims the settlement, and the expenses it was attributed to. A person who owes nothing is offered disabled with "share an expense first"; share the expense from its own row, then settle. The server still enforces that a repayment only reduces an expense shared with the payer, and anyone who has settled part of an expense stays in its split until that settlement moves.
- **Starting over.** Settings -> Admin -> Clear all splits and repayments removes every agreed split, repayment and deduction across all accounts and cash receipts, after a preview and confirmation and a recovery copy under `backups/admin`. Repayment receipts return to income when they carry income tags, otherwise to unsorted. Events keep their participants, so member expenses pick the event split up again when they join or participants change.
- **Older repayments.** Schema 22 records a split on every expense that took repayments before any was agreed: each payer's share is what they paid and the remainder is yours, marked "recorded from repayments". A recovery copy of the previous payloads is written under the private `backups/admin` folder first.
- **Tag.** The first tag takes the unsorted remainder. Another tag halves the largest tag, as before. Choosing a present tag removes it. After a row's first sort completes it, the list opens the next unsorted row (a checkbox in the heading turns this off). Later edits stay put.
- **Amounts.** Type an amount in a part row (Enter commits, Escape reverts). Cents always add up; the gap is never hidden. The strip in the header row has no handles any more; the amount boxes replaced them.
- **Move.** Opening a row highlights its likely twin in the list with a Same money button, and lists it first in the describe box as "To <account>" or "From <account>". Either side can start the link. Linking writes both sides through the versioned transfer API with the pair's own band; the toast reads "Moved between accounts" with the fee or extra, which also appear as parts. The linked card shows where the money went and Unlink.
- **Event.** The Event slot offers the four events nearest the row's date; the describe box finds the rest. An event adds context without changing amounts, including on linked transfers, and applies its participants' split when the expense joins it.
- **Batch.** Ctrl-click or Shift-click rows, then tag them together. Rows that are linked, already carry the tag or point the other way are skipped.
- **Focus.** Opening a row with an unsorted gap focuses the describe box. Opening a sorted row does not, so browsing does not steal the keyboard.
- **Undo.** Every save shows a toast with Undo; Ctrl+Z also works. Undo replays the previous parts through the same versioned saves, including relinking or unlinking a transfer.

## Smart organizing (October 4, 2026)

With Smart organizing on (Settings → Codex, or the switch in the heading), opening an unsorted row asks Codex what it is and shows the answer above the describe box: tags with amounts, a new tag when none fits, a transfer twin, a person settling up, people to share with, a payee and an event, each with a reason and a checkbox. Apply runs the same gestures as the describe box, so saves, Undo and the move to the next unsorted row behave as usual. Suggest for all unsorted, in the heading, asks about every unsorted row in the background; a star on each unsorted row shows grey (no suggestion), pulsing (asking) or filled (a suggestion is waiting), and the Suggested filter lists the waiting ones. A load overtaken by a newer one now waits for it, so a queued save never reads an older version than the ledger already on screen. See [Codex suggestions](codex.md).

## Semantics that did not change

Saves go through `saveAllocation`, `saveFinancial` (shares only), `followEventSplit`, `linkTransfer`, `unlinkTransfer` and `organize` with the row's current version; a stale version fails visibly and the ledger reloads. The model in `src/allocation-model.js` still owns exact-cent conservation, repayment caps, transfer eligibility and the unallocated remainder. Rules applied with manual review show "Suggested by a rule" and a Confirm button that files the decision. Legacy full-receipt tags on receipts with deductions keep their proportional note. Transfer eligibility still uses `pendingTransfers`: rows with shares or repayment reservations are never offered as twins.

Immediate save means there is no draft state and no Save button. A tag applied by mistake is one Undo away; nothing changes by browsing, hovering or opening a row.

## Transfer settings

Settings → Transfers hosts the account route network, the date distance and the amount tolerance that Organize uses to find twins, plus the preview and sandbox tools that used to live in the Auto-link lab. The open row states the current tolerance and links there. Twins on an allowed route rank first; other candidates within tolerance are still offered, so a manual choice can override the routes as before.

## Known limits

- Explicitly saving Other is not offered; the unsorted gap is the remainder. Records saved as Other by older screens appear as unsorted gaps.
- A transfer whose other account has not been imported yet has no pending state; the describe box shows the account disabled with the reason. Own accounts that are never imported as Move destinations, vendor refunds as Offsets and loans are part of the agreed model but not built yet.
- Manual cash receipts and financial purpose edits stay in Transactions; shares can also still be edited there.
- Twin matching compares against the full pending set on each open row; the Between accounts filter computes it for every row and is memoised per ledger load.

## Verification

`node scripts/smoke-organize-desk.cjs` runs the real app against an isolated synthetic workspace: tagging with advance, a split through typed amounts, an event that applies its participants' split, sharing from the describe box and from the Split slot, settling a balance across two shared expenses with oldest-first attribution and a taggable remainder, the settler and follow-event guards, linking from the list and from the describe box, unlink, undo, batch tagging and its undo, the Unsorted filter, the deep link from Transactions, the Settings → Transfers tab, the Admin clear, dark mode and an 820px layout. `scripts/smoke-navigation-refresh.cjs` covers the Transactions → Organize link, linking a twin and the Settings tabs. The older `smoke-allocations.cjs`, `smoke-allocation-drag.cjs`, `smoke-allocation-templates.cjs`, `smoke-rule-reuse.cjs` and `smoke-transfers.cjs` target the retired inbox-and-sheet layout and are reference-only until migrated. The disposable study that led here is in `prototypes/organize-desk/`.
